What happens on this page

Photo check and preview run entirely in your browser. Your photo is not uploaded while you check it or create the preview. To detect the face, your browser loads a face detection model from jsDelivr and Google (Google Cloud Storage). These providers see your IP address, as with any website request.

When you order

Only when you click “Order now” are the following sent: your photo, your e-mail address, the chosen board options, the language and the result of the photo check. Purpose: creating and delivering your string art and contacting you about your order. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of a contract).

  • Cloudflare, Inc. (USA) receives the order and forwards it (Cloudflare Workers).
  • Telegram (Telegram Messenger Inc.) delivers the order to the operator.
  • GitHub, Inc. (USA) computes the string art in a temporary virtual machine (GitHub Actions). Photo and results are deleted at the end of each run and are not stored in the repository.
  • Ko-fi (Ko-fi Labs Limited, UK) processes the payment under its own privacy policy.

Transfers to the USA are based on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and/or standard contractual clauses of the respective provider.
