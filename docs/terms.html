Terms of Service

Last updated: 2026-11-07

Story Reels Uploader ("the App") is a personal automation tool that generates short-form videos and publishes them, via the official TikTok Content Posting API and the official Instagram Graph API, to social media accounts owned and controlled by the developer.

Scope

  • The App only posts content to accounts explicitly authorized by their owner through each platform's own OAuth login flow.
  • The App does not act on behalf of, or access data belonging to, any third-party user or account.
  • The App is not offered as a public service, product or SaaS to other users. It is operated solely for the developer's own accounts.

Use of platform APIs

The App uses TikTok's and Meta's official developer APIs in accordance with each platform's Developer Terms of Service and Community Guidelines. Every video is shown to the account owner for review before it is published.

The App is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind. The developer is not liable for any interruption, suspension or change to third-party platform APIs the App depends on.
