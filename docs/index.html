Story Reels Uploader

A personal automation tool by 5hitcake.

Story Reels Uploader creates short story videos (true scary stories and historical mysteries) and publishes them to social media accounts owned by the developer: the Instagram accounts @wahre_gruselgeschichten and @imysteryhistoryi and the developer's own TikTok accounts.

How it uses TikTok

  • Login Kit (user.info.basic): the account owner logs in once with TikTok to authorize the tool for their own account.
  • Content Posting API (video.upload): finished videos are sent to the owner's TikTok inbox as drafts; the owner reviews and posts them in the TikTok app.
  • Content Posting API (video.publish): optionally, finished videos are published directly to the owner's own account.

The tool is not offered to other users and does not access anyone else's data. Source code: github.com/5hitcake/IGautomation.
